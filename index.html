💡 Why is sub-second latency important?
Human conversational pause in phone calls is typically between 600ms and 900ms. Because Groq provides ultra-fast inference and Kokoro-82M generates speech in < 200ms locally, the user hears the AI start speaking naturally without awkward dead-air pauses.
